    Become Partner / Sponsor / Donor

    Multiply impact across Indonesian springs

    We work with corporates, foundations, government bodies and community networks. Sponsor a region, fund a project type, or co-design a volunteer programme with us — outputs and locations are reported through verified field forms.

    • Endemic Tree Planting · Plant native species around a spring or its recharge area.
    • Trench (Rorak) Development · Build infiltration trenches that recharge groundwater.
    • Spring Restoration · Remove sediment and rehabilitate a degraded spring.
    • Monitoring Expedition · Field expedition to inventory and assess springs.
